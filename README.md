# Faces by Victoria

# Faces By Victoria Simple Landing Page

Build a **simple, premium, mobile-first one-page website** for **Faces By Victoria**, an aesthetics and skin specialist in Gibraltar.

This is **not** intended to be a large clinic website.

The goal is simply to give Faces By Victoria a polished professional website presence, clearly explain what Victoria offers, and direct visitors to:

* **Fresha to book**
* **Instagram and other social channels**
* Contact / WhatsApp if required

Victoria's social channels will remain the primary place for ongoing content, treatment examples, results, updates and engagement.

The website should therefore stay **very simple, fast and visually strong**.

---

# Core Objective

A visitor should be able to understand within a few seconds:

**Who is Faces By Victoria?**

**What does Victoria offer?**

**Where is she based?**

**How do I book?**

**Where can I follow her?**

Do not create a large multi-page clinic website.

Do not create individual treatment pages.

Do not create a large results gallery.

Do not create a blog.

Do not create a CMS.

Do not create an internal booking system.

Everything should exist on **one clean landing page**.

---

# Brand

Faces By Victoria is centred entirely around **Victoria**.

Do not mention Ian anywhere.

Do not include any services that belonged specifically to Ian.

The site's colours and styling should remain consistent with the existing **Faces By Victoria logo, Instagram and social branding**.

Use the existing brand aesthetic rather than trying to introduce an entirely new identity.

The overall feeling should be:

* Premium
* Modern
* Feminine without being overly girly
* Clean
* Confident
* Professional
* Warm
* Personal

Avoid making it look like a generic beauty salon.

---

# Navigation

Keep the navigation extremely small.

Desktop:

**FACES BY VICTORIA**

Treatments
About
Contact

**BOOK NOW**

The navigation links should simply scroll to sections on the same page.

**BOOK NOW** should link directly to Victoria's Fresha booking page.

On mobile:

Use a simple compact menu or minimal full-screen menu.

Make booking especially easy on mobile.

Consider a persistent mobile **Book Now** button at the bottom of the screen.

---

# Landing Page Structure

The whole website should contain approximately **5 core sections**.

Do not add unnecessary sections simply to make the page longer.

---

# 1. Hero

Create a clean premium hero section.

Use either:

* A strong professional image of Victoria
* A beautiful Faces By Victoria treatment/skin image
* Or a restrained combination of Victoria and existing brand imagery

Keep text minimal.

### Copy

**FACES BY VICTORIA**

Headline:

**Aesthetics, refined.**

Supporting copy:

**Expert-led aesthetics, skin rejuvenation and age-management in Gibraltar.**

Primary button:

**BOOK NOW**

This links directly to Fresha.

Secondary link:

**Follow on Instagram**

Do not overload the hero with information.

On mobile, Victoria's name, the value proposition and booking button should all appear clearly without excessive scrolling.

---

# 2. Treatments

Create one simple section explaining Victoria's main areas of treatment.

Headline:

**Treatments**

Intro:

**Bespoke aesthetic and skin treatments designed around you.**

Do not list every individual service available in Fresha.

Instead use a small number of broad categories.

For example:

### Anti-Wrinkle

Treatments designed to soften lines while maintaining natural facial movement.

### Dermal Fillers

Subtle enhancement and facial balancing, including lips, cheeks and facial contouring.

### Skin & Rejuvenation

Skin boosters, advanced skincare and treatments focused on improving skin quality and rejuvenation.

### PDO Threads

Non-surgical treatments designed to support lifting and facial rejuvenation.

### Body Treatments

Fat-dissolving and body-contouring treatments currently offered by Victoria.

Only include categories that Victoria currently provides.

Each category does **not** need its own page.

At the bottom of the section include:

**View all treatments & prices on Fresha →**

This should link directly to the Fresha service menu.

Fresha remains the source of truth for:

* Exact treatments
* Current pricing
* Appointment availability
* Promotions
* Booking

---

# 3. About Victoria

Keep this concise.

Use a strong photograph of Victoria.

Small heading:

**ABOUT VICTORIA**

Large headline:

**Experience matters.**

Add a short paragraph introducing Victoria, her experience and her treatment philosophy.

The emphasis should be on:

* Extensive experience within aesthetics and skin
* Natural-looking results
* Facial balance
* Skin quality
* Personalised treatment
* Long-term rejuvenation rather than following trends

Do not create a long biography.

Do not mention Ian.

Add a small CTA:

**Book with Victoria →**

Link to Fresha.

---

# 4. Social / Results

Victoria's social channels are the main place where clients should discover:

* Before and after results
* Treatment videos
* New treatments
* Offers
* Educational content
* Behind-the-scenes content

Therefore do **not** create a large website results gallery.

Instead create a visually strong social section.

Headline:

**See more on Instagram**

Show either:

* 3 to 6 Instagram-style image tiles
* Or a simple curated image strip

Include:

**@faces.byvictoria**

Button:

**FOLLOW ON INSTAGRAM**

If a live Instagram feed introduces unnecessary complexity, use static images that link to Instagram instead.

Keep it technically simple.

---

# 5. Contact & Booking

Make the final section extremely clear.

Headline:

**Ready to book?**

Supporting text:

**View Victoria's treatments, live availability and current pricing on Fresha.**

Large button:

**BOOK ON FRESHA**

Then show:

**Faces By Victoria**
17–21 Cannon Lane
Gibraltar

Social/contact icons for:

* Instagram
* Facebook if applicable
* WhatsApp if applicable
* Fresha

Keep this section clean and simple.

---

# Footer

Very small footer.

Include:

**Faces By Victoria**

Gibraltar

Instagram
Fresha

Privacy Policy
Cookie Policy

Copyright.

No oversized multi-column footer.

---

# Visual Style

Use Victoria's existing branding as the starting point.

Match the existing Faces By Victoria logo and social media colour palette.

Do not radically reinvent the brand.

The design can still feel more premium through:

* Better typography
* More whitespace
* High-quality imagery
* Large photographs
* Restrained animation
* Clean alignment

Avoid:

* Excessive cards
* Huge amounts of text
* Complex menus
* Multiple page layouts
* Heavy animations
* Generic clinic icons
* Large feature grids
* Pricing tables
* Blog-style content
* Fake awards or statistics

---

# Typography

Use one elegant serif or refined display font for large headings and a clean sans-serif for everything else.

Suggested combination:

**Instrument Serif** — headings

**Inter** — navigation, buttons and body copy

Typography should feel modern and premium but remain very readable on mobile.

---

# Mobile Experience

Mobile is extremely important.

Design **mobile first**.

The majority of visitors are likely to arrive from:

* Instagram
* Facebook
* Google
* Shared links

The mobile experience should therefore be extremely quick.

On mobile:

1. Show Victoria / brand immediately
2. Clearly state what she does
3. Display a prominent **BOOK NOW** button
4. Make treatments easy to scan
5. Make Instagram easy to reach
6. Keep scrolling relatively short

Avoid excessive padding that makes users scroll through huge empty sections.

Buttons should be large enough to tap easily.

---

# Booking

Do not create booking functionality.

Every booking CTA should link to the existing Faces By Victoria Fresha page.

Use Fresha for:

* Services
* Prices
* Availability
* Booking
* Promotions
* Packages or memberships where applicable

The website is simply the polished front door.

**Website → understand Victoria → Fresha → book.**

---

# Social Strategy

The website should deliberately avoid duplicating Victoria's social content.

Social media remains the active content channel.

The website should instead provide:

**Credibility + brand presence + treatment overview + booking.**

Instagram handles:

**Results + videos + education + updates + personality + ongoing content.**

Make the Instagram link prominent throughout the website.

---

# Technical Requirements

Use:

* React
* TypeScript
* Tailwind CSS

Keep the build lightweight.

One landing page only.

No database.

No CMS.

No account system.

No ecommerce.

No internal booking engine.

No dynamic results system.

No complex integrations.

Optimise all images.

Lazy load imagery below the fold.

Ensure excellent mobile performance and Core Web Vitals.

---

# Basic SEO

Even though the site is simple, implement good local SEO.

Homepage title example:

**Faces By Victoria | Aesthetics & Skin Treatments Gibraltar**

Meta description:

**Faces By Victoria provides expert-led aesthetic, skin rejuvenation and age-management treatments in Gibraltar. View treatments and book online.**

Naturally reference:

* Aesthetics Gibraltar
* Aesthetic treatments Gibraltar
* Skin treatments Gibraltar
* Anti-wrinkle treatments Gibraltar
* Dermal fillers Gibraltar

Do not keyword-stuff.

Add appropriate LocalBusiness / BeautyBusiness structured data if suitable.

---

# Final Design Principle

This website should **not try to replace Fresha or Instagram**.

It should simply make Faces By Victoria look credible, premium and professional when someone searches for the business or clicks through from social media.

The ideal visitor journey is:

**Land on website → understand Victoria and her services → trust the brand → book on Fresha or follow on Instagram.**

Keep it short.

Keep it beautiful.

Keep it fast.

Keep booking obvious.
